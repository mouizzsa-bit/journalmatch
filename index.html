How the money works. Wiley, Elsevier, Springer and Taylor & Francis waive fees only on fully open-access journals, never hybrid ones. If your country is Tier A (Kenya, Benin + 75 others) you typically pay nothing and get open access on a participating fully-OA journal. A 🔒 hybrid journal gives no waiver on its OA option — so we show its free subscription route (paywalled) as the headline and note the paid OA price beneath.
